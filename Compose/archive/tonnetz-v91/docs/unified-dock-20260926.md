# Unified Tonnetz performance dock

- Settings, playback, Auto/Hold, four chord labels, quick style/BPM, and Accompaniment expansion now share one 56 px row.
- Removed the separate current-chord and Start/Stop strip.
- Both drawers open above the persistent row; switching between them closes the other. Settings can also be closed from its row button.
- Small screens keep the four chord labels and primary controls; the detailed drawer retains tempo/style and all other parameters.

Validation: 48 existing Tonnetz checks pass. Local browser layout inspected at 1280×720, 1024×700 and 390×700. Verified drawer switching, Learn scrolling, quick style/BPM sync, accompaniment audio activity, automatic progression across bar boundaries, Hold and Stop.
