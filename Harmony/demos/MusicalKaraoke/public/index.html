Why the circle of fifths closes

C → G · count C–D–E–F–G

Pure fifth

Just intonation uses simple frequency ratios. An octave doubles the frequency. In a pure fifth, G makes three vibrations while C makes two: a 3:2 ratio.

C · G · D · A · E · B · F♯ · C♯ · G♯ · D♯ · A♯ · F · C

Twelve fifths

Stacking pure fifths is Pythagorean tuning. Twelve pure fifths do not equal seven octaves; the mismatch is the Pythagorean comma.

Twelve equal steps per octave

12-tone equal temperament

Each semitone uses the same frequency ratio. A fifth spans seven semitones; twelve fifths return to C, ignoring octaves.
